🧹 Clean up your Android TV

These steps are for compatible Android TV and Google TV devices. Menu names differ by manufacturer.

  1. Open Settings → Apps → See all apps.
  2. Select an app you recognise and no longer need.
  3. If available, choose Uninstall or Disable. Some system apps cannot be removed.
  4. Open Settings → Storage, if available, to check free space.
  5. Restart the TV and check whether performance improves.
Safety: do not disable system services randomly. This website does not run ADB commands or modify your TV automatically.

📱 Connect your phone to your TV

  1. Connect the phone and TV to the same local Wi-Fi network.
  2. Check the TV brand and exact model in TV Settings → About.
  3. Check whether the manufacturer supports network control, remote-control pairing, or Android Debug Bridge (ADB).
  4. Use only a compatible, trusted connection method and approve pairing on the TV itself.

Opening this dashboard on a phone does not automatically discover or control TVs. A browser cannot freely scan your local network or execute privileged TV commands.

🛠️ Picture troubleshooting

Picture looks too dark

Check brightness, backlight or OLED pixel brightness, power-saving settings and ambient-light settings.

Colours look incorrect

Try the standard or cinema picture mode. Check colour temperature, HDMI settings and source-device output.

Lines, flickering or dead pixels

Try another input or source and restart the TV. Persistent defects may require professional service.

🎨 Screen test patterns

Use the patterns to look for visible irregularities. For a valid assessment, display them on the TV itself, ideally at its native resolution. Browser scaling and video processing can affect results.
